The idea behind krako.xyz

The world already owns enough computers. Most of them are asleep.

Your phone does almost nothing for the six to eight hours you are asleep. Your laptop sits closed. The old tablet in the drawer has not been switched on in months. Every one of those devices is real computing power that someone already paid for, idle by default.

Krako.xyz is built on a simple idea: let people rent out that idle time. You opt in, choose the hours you genuinely do not need your device, and it joins a distributed network that runs real workloads while you sleep. You earn from capacity you were never using. The network gets compute without anyone pouring concrete for another data centre.

KORA is the layer that makes this practical. It breaks work into deterministic task graphs — pieces small enough to run across ordinary consumer devices — and only escalates to a full model when a model is genuinely needed. Less waste on both ends.
